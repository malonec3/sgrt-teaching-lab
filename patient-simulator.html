Why

Numbers and how this is modelled
About this model. Synthetic supine patient built from smooth shapes, seen as a height map from directly above — the view an overhead camera has. Registration is point-to-plane ICP on the surface inside the ROI (translations, optionally with rotations about the ROI centre); directions the surface cannot constrain are left uncorrected rather than guessed. No camera occlusion, no colour texture, no internal anatomy, no vendor algorithm. Magnitudes are illustrative; the mechanisms are the lesson. Context: AAPM TG-302 (2022).
